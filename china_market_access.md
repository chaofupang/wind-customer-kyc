# China Market Access Reference

## QFII

Use the bundled `qfii_emea_reference.xlsx` as an internal high-potential-customer reference.

A confirmed legal-name match is Tier 1 evidence of China market access.

Do not treat fuzzy name similarity alone as confirmation. If a parent/subsidiary relationship is involved, verify the legal relationship first.

## Bond Connect Northbound

Use the latest official approved-investor list when web access is available:

https://www.chinabondconnect.com/sc/Northbound/Onboarding/Approved-Investors.html

A confirmed match is Tier 1 evidence of China fixed-income market access.

## Scoring principle

- QFII confirmed → strong uplift to China relevance and China equity/PIT/API fit.
- Bond Connect confirmed → strong uplift to China relevance and China bond/credit/valuation/API fit.
- Both confirmed → China relevance normally at least 8/10 unless there is strong evidence the access/business is inactive.
